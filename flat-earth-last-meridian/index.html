    EXPEDITION BRIEF // DISC YEAR 611

    The Sun Track is slipping.

    The low Sun circles above the Disc, carrying a moving island of warmth and daylight with it. Three meridian stations have gone dark. You have a 05:30 correction window before the Rim Observatory loses the track and the long freeze reaches the outer settlements.

    Your survey sledge is solar-assisted, heat-limited, and light enough for the rim wind to push. Follow the Sun when you need power. Use darkness when frozen survey lanes become fast. Under the rotating firmament, take star fixes to cancel navigation drift.

    DAY MOVESThe Sun is a nearby moving light, not a distant global sunrise.
    NIGHT FREEZESDark survey lanes harden into shortcuts, while your heat drains.
    THE RIM PUSHESCold outward winds strengthen near the encircling Ice Wall.
    STARS TURNThe firmament rotates around the central pole; night fixes tame rim drift.

    This is an alternate-world game whose fictional physics deliberately follow flat-Earth cosmology; it is not a scientific model of the real Earth.
