- Presentation-only runtime that synthesizes, optionally hash-checks, decodes, resolves, admits, and plays declared slices through UFR-124; preload failures are observable and retryable.

- Review-bank build command, deterministic verifier, authoritative verification stage, focused tests, and UI-SFX ownership documentation.
