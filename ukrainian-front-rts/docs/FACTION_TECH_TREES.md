# Faction technology-tree contract

UFR-070 defines a versioned, deterministic faction contract in `src/content/faction-tech-trees.js`.

Both factions expose production structures, complete roster slots, prerequisite-linked nodes, explicit counter relationships, doctrine identity, and a unique mechanic. Ukraine follows networked maneuver with a shared target network; Russia follows echeloned pressure with operational mass. Shared role names are contracts for later roster tasks, not permission to mirror cost, health, or damage values.

The validator rejects unsupported schema versions, duplicate IDs, missing prerequisites, missing counter relationships, incomplete roster coverage, and missing unique mechanics. Later UFR-071 through UFR-078 tasks extend these stable IDs with full content and systems.
