# Research notes

These notes document the real-world observations used as the factual substrate of the fictional campaign. The conspiracy plot is intentionally invented.

## Santiago (SCL) ↔ Sydney (SYD)

As of August 2026, direct passenger service exists between Santiago and Sydney.

Useful current references:

- Qantas — Santiago to Sydney offers and route page:
  https://www.qantas.com/cl/en/flight-deals/flights-from-santiago-to-sydney.html/scl/syd/economy
- LATAM — Australia destinations from Santiago, including direct Sydney offers:
  https://www.latamairlines.com/cl/es/destinos/australia
- FlightsFrom route summary (secondary schedule aggregator): approximately 11,401 km and roughly 15 h 15 m scheduled:
  https://www.flightsfrom.com/SCL-SYD
- DirectFlights route summary (secondary schedule aggregator): current schedules show roughly 13 h 10 m to 15 h 15 m depending on service/date:
  https://www.directflights.com/SCL-SYD

The game deliberately describes the duration as a range ("about 14–15 hours") rather than pretending one schedule value is universal. Winds, exact track, carrier schedule and direction change block time.

### Why this is useful in the story

On the common north-pole-centred azimuthal-equidistant graphic used in many flat-Earth communities, southern intercontinental distances are strongly distorted. A real, repeatedly flown southern route therefore makes a better investigation clue than a single screenshot or authority quote: time, distance, aircraft performance, passenger clocks and independent schedules all have to remain mutually compatible.

The game does **not** claim that a map screenshot by itself proves anything.

## Drain / Coriolis myth

Reference:

- Library of Congress, Everyday Mysteries:
  https://www.loc.gov/everyday-mysteries/item/does-water-go-down-the-drain-counterclockwise-in-the-northern-hemisphere-and-clockwise-in-the-southern-hemisphere/

For sinks, toilets and bathtubs, geometry and how the water was introduced dominate; the Coriolis effect is too weak to make drain direction a reliable hemisphere test. The campaign includes this as a deliberately bad answer in an evidence-quality puzzle.
