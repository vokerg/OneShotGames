# Research notes — factual substrate vs fictional conspiracy

These notes document the real-world observations used by **Flat Earth II // Southern Circuit**. The intelligence/conspiracy layer is deliberately fictional. The game uses conspiracy aesthetics as a thriller device, but does not present invented material as a real allegation.

## 1. Santiago (SCL) → Sydney (SYD)

The campaign begins on a real southern intercontinental route.

Current / useful references:

- Qantas Santiago airport page lists QF27/QF28 operations:
  https://www.qantas.com/au/en/qantas-experience/at-the-airport/airport-guides/santiago-airport-guide.html
- Flight.info current QF28 schedule data: roughly 14 h 30 m and about 11,401 km:
  https://www.flight.info/QF28
- Airportia QF28 current route statistics provide a similar order of distance and scheduled block time:
  https://www.airportia.com/flights/qf28/santiago/sydney/

The game treats exact block time as variable. Wind, routing, date and schedule matter. The clue is the **order of magnitude and repeated operational consistency**, not one screenshot or one exact number.

### Why this matters to the game

A north-pole-centred azimuthal-equidistant graphic commonly reused in flat-Earth material strongly stretches southern east-west distances. A repeatedly operated SCL→SYD route therefore creates a useful model constraint: route length, elapsed time, plausible aircraft speed, fuel, passenger clocks and independent schedules must all fit together.

## 2. Southern celestial pole

Useful reference:

- EarthSky, star trails and celestial-pole geometry:
  https://earthsky.org/astronomy-essentials/what-are-star-trails/

In the Southern Hemisphere, long-exposure star trails circle the south celestial pole. There is no comparably bright southern equivalent of Polaris marking the pole. The campaign deliberately distinguishes an aircraft-window timelapse (weak clue) from a stationary repeated observation (stronger procedure).

## 3. Antarctica is regulated, not an inaccessible ring-wall

Official Antarctic Treaty material:

- Antarctic Treaty Secretariat — Treaty overview:
  https://www.ats.aq/e/antarctictreaty.html
- Peaceful use and inspections:
  https://www.ats.aq/e/peaceful.html
- Information exchange requirements:
  https://www.ats.aq/e/information.html
- Tourism / visitor inspection material appears in the Treaty system, for example Resolution 11 (2012):
  https://www.ats.aq/devAS/Meetings/Measure/525

Article VII provides inspection rights to designated observers, including access to areas, stations, installations, ships and aircraft in relevant Antarctic locations. This is materially different from a claim that the continent is universally sealed off from observation.

Current tourism statistics:

- International Association of Antarctica Tour Operators (IAATO), 2025–26 data:
  https://iaato.org/news-room/data-statistics

IAATO reports 27,217 cruise-only visitors, 85,195 landed visits and 1,106 deep-field visitors for 2025–26. The game is careful not to equate tourism with unrestricted access: Antarctica is remote, expensive, environmentally regulated and logistically difficult.

## 5. Shadows / Eratosthenes-style geometry

NASA Science (updated March 2026) describes how Eratosthenes compared solstice shadows at different locations and used the angular difference plus the distance between locations to estimate Earth's circumference:

- https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/skywatching/night-sky-network/tropical-solstice-shadows/

The game includes a simplified numerical puzzle: a 7.2° difference is 1/50 of a circle; if the locations are roughly 800 km apart, the implied circumference is roughly 40,000 km.

The important point in the game is not reverence for the historical name, but that **simultaneous shadow geometry can be repeated by independent observers**.

## 7. Local-Sun / perspective claim, angular size and refraction

NASA describes the Sun as subtending roughly half a degree in the sky:

- https://science.nasa.gov/learn/basics-of-space-flight/chapter1-1/

NOAA explains that atmospheric refraction shifts the Sun's apparent position near the horizon and makes apparent sunrise slightly earlier / sunset slightly later:

- https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/glossary.html

The campaign does not pretend that one phone photograph settles solar geometry. Instead the SUNSET FILE asks the player to preserve exposure settings, compare angular size, timing and position, and explicitly account for near-horizon refraction.

## 8. Long-distance water / laser observations

Internet arguments about distant objects over water often hinge on camera height, target height, distance, atmospheric layering and refraction. The WATERLINE section deliberately teaches **experimental controls**, not a magic one-video proof.

NOAA's solar-refraction material above is included as a clear official example that the atmosphere bends apparent lines of sight and that pressure/humidity conditions affect the exact magnitude. In the game, a single dramatic laser/video clip is therefore classified as an observation needing geometry and atmospheric metadata before it can carry strong weight.

## 9. Flat-Earth claims represented in the campaign

The game uses broad claim families that genuinely occur in flat-Earth discourse, but avoids attributing a composite claim to a specific living person unless necessary. They include:

- southern routes are impossible / fabricated;
- Antarctica functions as a forbidden ice wall;
- 24-hour Antarctic sunlight cannot be observed;
- southern celestial motion can be dismissed as an agency-generated image;
- sink rotation is a hemisphere proof;
- all globe evidence depends on NASA or one authority;
- a north-pole-centred flat map can be treated as a globally consistent distance model;
- sunsets can be explained purely by a nearby Sun receding in perspective;
- a single long-distance water/laser video is definitive;
- secret-looking documents should receive more epistemic weight because they are secret-looking.

The point of the campaign is to force these claims to coexist in **one compatible model**. HORIZON's fictional manipulation works by preventing that compatibility test.
